# L6 — Causal Mathematical Reality Equilibrium

L6 is the decisive supplied-formal-semantics test of whether a compiled
mathematical reality causally shapes a movable latent equilibrium. It reuses
the audited formal expression types from I3/L4 but does not reuse L5's exact
consumer-key activation path.

## Authority boundary

Exact records validate reality, scope, time, provenance and legal mathematical
transitions. Continuous factor activations, source-normalized support and
opposition, alternatives, final candidate selection and uncertainty belong to
the registered latent field law. The independent evaluator replays every
accepted derivation and computes the expected weighted equilibrium separately.

The prompt contains assumptions and a query slot, never an answer, target,
route, depth, required body or evaluator field. A field body can be retrieved
from minimap compatibility, but its outcomes are never inserted as exact
active keys. Outputs remain soft and factual operations are always empty.

## Locked design

The locked field is planned to contain 25,000 query-independent bodies: 15,000
standard mathematical bodies and 2,000 bodies in each of five signed custom
realities. Standard and custom realities are isolated. Contradictions use a
paraconsistent weighted law: both sides survive, independent source mass and
relational support determine the primary mode, and balanced modes decode as
winner-plus-tension or alternatives.

The authoritative query suite has 8,000 cases with exact depths 1–20,
weighted contradictions, balanced alternatives, unknowns, context isolation
and standard/custom counterfactual twins. One registered body application is
one hop. The controlled-text compiler is a separate diagnostic and cannot
create or erase the formal L6 classification.

## Required result

`L6-A` requires perfect accepted precision, independent replay, exact depth-20
success of at least 0.85, contradiction and alternative gates, and causal
advantages of at least 0.20 over no geometry/fixed state and 0.30 over random
geometry. If those controls fail, the result is `L6-D` even if deterministic
field propagation is accurate.
