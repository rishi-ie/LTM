# G4 — Prompt-Conditioned Active Frontier Experiment

## Objective

Determine whether typed traversal from correct topology addresses can recover
all registered answer-changing factors while opening less than one percent of a
100,000-factor topology.

The authoritative test uses gold starting addresses. G3 composition is a
separate diagnostic. G4 records open obligations and omissions but does not
certify them as harmless; coverage certification and widening remain G5.

## Fixed experiment

- 25,000 development factors and 120 cases;
- 100,000 locked factors and 300 cases;
- six balanced traversal families;
- path depth one through six;
- 512-factor, 64-block, depth-eight frontier budget;
- exhaustive symbolic conclusion control;
- CPU-only, offline, under ten minutes and two GB RSS.

## Pass boundary

G4-A requires at least 99% required-factor recall, 98% exhaustive conclusion
agreement, perfect hard-constraint and exception recall, at least 99% session,
bridge, conflict and provenance recall, no false resolved conclusion, less than
one percent median opened factors, and deterministic reproduction.

A pass authorizes G5 only. It does not repair the compiler, prove latent
optimization, or establish large-context reliability.
