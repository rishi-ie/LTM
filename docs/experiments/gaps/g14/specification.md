# G14 — Unified Benchmark and Diagnostic Evaluation

G14 evaluates the integrated architecture with two intentionally separate
verdicts. The controlled verdict composes the registered structured components
over 50 locked 12-turn conversations. The product verdict evaluates whether the
currently frozen raw-language compiler and decoder boundaries are usable on the
official LongMemEval and LoCoMo datasets.

Published baseline scores are contextual only. Locked local gates use only
reproducible deterministic metrics and preserve the known G2/G2.1/G10 failures.
