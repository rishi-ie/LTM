# G2.7 — Frozen Semantic Reasoning-Atom Coordinate Compiler

G2.7 tests whether frozen `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` plus a compact, trainable topology kernel can preserve an 18-dimensional reasoning-atom coordinate, bind content to G1 roles, and compile controlled unseen language into exact G1 and FieldIR. G1 sparse topology is authoritative; vectors provide routing and field geometry.

The experiment is fail-fast. The gold-content kernel is evaluated before span extraction, identity resolution or document composition. The operational encoder is frozen and hashed. A passing result requires accepted exact precision of at least 95%, safe coverage of at least 90%, and zero accepted direction/polarity reversals or invalid insertions.

The executable atom bank is exactly the current G1 registry. Higher-level notions such as “why,” “how” and “solution” are represented as validated compositions of G1 relations. No G1–G15 historical result is changed.

Commands are exposed through `python -m topology_g27`; generated data remains under the ignored G2.7 workspace. A failed kernel refuses freeze and locked evaluation and is recorded as `G2.7-B`.
