# LTM v1 Evidence Boundary

> Normative contract: [LTM-ARCH-1.2](architecture-lock-v1.md).

The results ledger is the only status authority. Experiment reports remain the
source of measured details. This document states what the architecture may
claim from those results.

## Supported by controlled evidence

- G1 exact topology representation, validation, persistence and replay.
- G3–G5 structured addressing, sparse frontier construction and coverage
  certificates.
- G6 exact registered relation propagation.
- G7 soft reconciliation after immutable hard reasoning.
- G8 order-independent memory-bounded reduction.
- G9 independent bundle verification.
- G10.1 strict authorized surface realization.
- G11 session lifecycle and assistant-event non-contamination.
- G12 persistent incremental storage.
- G13 controlled sparse scale layout.
- G14 controlled composition of the structured path.
- LTM-R1 compatibility of the numeric text-free active representation on its
  audit fixtures.
- LTM-I1 preservation of confirmed FieldIR v2 through G3–G10.1.
- LTM-R2 universal Mumbrane representation, four locked purpose profiles,
  tiered switching, packed reload and exact G1 projection on evaluator-owned
  semantic bodies.
- G2.14 supplied-span conversational routing with `1.0000` accepted precision,
  `0.9998` safe coverage, zero incorrect accepted mutations and exact
  conditional G11 lifecycle agreement.
- L1 independently replayed grounded formal and opaque traversal chains through
  64 hops with zero invalid accepted proofs. This is a capacity diagnostic, not
  evidence for arbitrary 64-hop mathematics.
- L7 `r3` fixed-law equilibrium over 240 supplied-formal prompts and one
  immutable 512-body acyclic field: `1.0000` exactness, accepted precision,
  depth-20 success and independent fixed-point agreement, with zero incorrect
  accepted conclusions. This is bounded equilibrium evidence, not general
  mathematical reasoning.

The architecture is therefore supported as a controlled post-transformer core:
its persistent field, exact execution, fixed-law optimization and verification
do not require transformer state as reasoning authority.

## Provisional or unproven

- G2.5 is the adopted compiler baseline, but its locked exact recovery was
  81.75% and it recorded 199 reversal false accepts; it remains the provisional
  reasoning route.
- G2.14 does not extract raw semantic spans and does not compile deep reasoning
  topology.
- G2.3 has no locked classification.
- G10’s original free decoder was model-limited; G10.1 is a strict constrained
  realization result, not a fluent general decoder.
- G13 does not prove arbitrary factor placement or production throughput.
- G14 does not prove raw-language product behavior.
- G15 serving, isolation and operational fault handling remain unrun.
- Cyclic, scaled, 64-hop and natural-language fixed-law equilibrium remain
  unresolved. L7 resolves only the supplied-formal acyclic depth-20 boundary.
- LTM-R2 did not freshly rerun G11–G14 lifecycle suites; their Mumbrane
  compatibility currently relies on exact G1 projection plus their separate
  locked evidence.
- The validated Mumbrane package remains isolated under `ltm_r2`; product
  promotion into `ltm` is not yet complete.
- Ordinary mathematical-language compilation (L2) has a conservative arithmetic
  development baseline but no locked result and remains untested as a full compiler.
- L3 passed controlled exact prose/notation compilation into a 50,000-body
  standard-mathematics field and verified `256 / 256` shortest 45-hop grounded
  proofs. It does not establish raw language, branching theorem discovery, or
  learned scorer necessity on that mostly linear corpus.
- L4 tested the missing branching boundary on a 12-case stratified pre-lock
  panel and stopped before lock. Accepted
  proofs remained exact, but answerable success was 33.3%, proposal recall@16
  was 27.4%, and scorer/goal/value controls failed. Learned branching proof
  discovery therefore remains unsupported.
- L8 is a development-only 16-observation reduced probe. It recorded 1.00
  independent equilibrium agreement and policy-twin divergence with zero
  trainable parameters and zero incorrect accepted conclusions. It supports
  the policy-conditioned mechanism provisionally, not general post-transformer
  reasoning or the full L8 suite.

## Safety policy

The first LTM build must be able to abstain, quarantine, request confirmation,
and expose provenance. It must not present the provisional compiler as a
reliable unrestricted-language parser.
